Shock Waves in Porous Conduits

Investigating the Influence of Porous Wall Geometry on Shock Wave Behavior

Learn More

Research Overview

This research investigates how the internal geometry of porous walls affects the behavior and propagation of shock waves moving through conduits. Understanding these interactions is crucial for applications in engineering, combustion, and fluid dynamics.

Focus Area

Porous wall geometry effects on shock wave dynamics

Applications

Industrial processes, combustion engines, safety systems

Field

Fluid Dynamics & Engineering
