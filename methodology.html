Research Methodology

Approach Overview

This research employs a multi-faceted approach combining experimental testing, computational simulations, and theoretical analysis to understand shock wave behavior in porous conduits.

Experimental Methods

Shock Tube Experiments

Controlled laboratory experiments using shock tubes with various porous wall configurations. Pressure and temperature measurements are taken at multiple points along the conduit to track shock wave propagation and attenuation.

  • High-speed pressure sensors for real-time data acquisition
  • Various porous material samples with different geometries
  • Temperature monitoring using thermocouples
  • High-speed imaging for visual analysis

Material Characterization

Detailed analysis of porous wall samples to understand their structural properties and how these properties affect shock wave interactions.

  • Porosity measurements using standard techniques
  • Pore size distribution analysis
  • Microscopic imaging of internal geometry
  • Permeability testing

Computational Methods

Numerical Simulations

Advanced computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations to model shock wave propagation through porous media. Multiple simulation scenarios are run to validate experimental findings and explore parameter variations.

  • Compressible flow equations with shock-capturing schemes
  • Porous media modeling through effective medium theory
  • Mesh refinement in shock regions for accuracy
  • Time-accurate transient analysis

Parameter Studies

Systematic variation of key parameters to understand their individual and combined effects on shock wave behavior.

  • Pore size variation
  • Porosity percentage changes
  • Material composition variations
  • Initial shock strength variations

Data Analysis

Experimental and computational data are analyzed using advanced statistical methods and signal processing techniques to extract meaningful insights about shock wave-porous media interactions.

  • Pressure profile analysis and shock identification
  • Energy dissipation calculation
  • Wave speed determination
  • Attenuation rate quantification
  • Comparative analysis of experimental vs. computational results

Research Timeline

Phase 1: Experimental Setup

Equipment calibration and baseline experiments with smooth-wall controls

Phase 2: Material Testing

Systematic testing of various porous wall configurations

Phase 3: Computational Modeling

Development and validation of numerical models

Phase 4: Data Analysis

Comprehensive analysis and model refinement

Phase 5: Results & Publication

Final findings and research dissemination
